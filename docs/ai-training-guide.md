# Optional AI training alongside deployment

This is a facilitator-led exercise for small-business staff, not a record of
completed client training. Use only the synthetic application and invented notes.
Do not upload customer records, credentials or internal documents to an AI tool.

## Exercise

1. Facilitator runs the local synthetic app and provides its inventory summary.
2. Learner asks an approved AI tool to draft a short stock update from that summary.
3. Learner checks every item, quantity and monetary value against the source.
4. Facilitator introduces a draft containing a wrong quantity and an unsupported
   delivery promise. Learner marks both errors and revises the draft.
5. Learner identifies which actions require a person: sending the update,
   changing inventory, promising delivery and handling private customer data.
6. Learner writes a handoff explaining what was checked and what is still unknown.

## Assessment

Record whether each factual claim was sourced, whether both inserted errors were
caught, whether private data was excluded, and whether the draft stayed unsent.
Measure elapsed time and corrections during a real consented pilot before
claiming productivity gains. There are no invented before/after results here.

## Deployment handoff

Teach staff to read the health result, recognize the disposable demo mode and
contact the support owner. Backup and restore are operator tasks, not something
an AI-generated instruction should execute without review. Keep a written
escalation contact and verified recovery procedure with the deployed system.
